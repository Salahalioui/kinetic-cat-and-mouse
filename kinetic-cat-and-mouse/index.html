HOW TO PLAY

🏃 Movement & Fast-Fall

A/D to sprint. W or Space for Jump and mid-air Double-Jump. Press S or Down in mid-air to trigger a Fast-Fall Ground Slam!

⚡ Supersonic Dash

Press Shift to dash forward. During Dash, you are completely immune to Laser Spikes! Be careful: GM absorbs +15 NRG per dash.

🛡️ Shield Barrier Orbs

Ascend to High Lanes to collect glowing green Shield Orbs! Each orb grants a 1-hit protective energy barrier that absorbs damage and heals HP.

🚪 Extraction Portal (EXIT DOOR)

Reach the giant golden glowing Gateway at the end of the runway to successfully complete the level and bank your clear time!

🎯 Universal Magnetic Snapping

All traps (Spikes, Ice, Wind, Phantom, Spring, Laser Gate) automatically snap vertically to the platform top surface under the cursor. Effortless 1-click placement!

🛡️ Safe-Zone Halo (220px)

A translucent circle surrounds the Hero at all times. You cannot build inside the halo; you must look down the track and set up predictive ambushes.

⚡ Overclocking & Traps

Hotkeys 1-6. Left-click an active Repulsor Fan or Laser Gate to Overclock it for 3 seconds of devastating speed!

🔄 Auto-Salvage Vacuum

Whenever the deadly Pacing Wall passes an old trap, it is automatically recycled, refunding 50% energy directly to your bank.

⏱️ Stopwatch Role-Swap Format

Matches consist of 2 rounds. In Round 1, Player 1 is Hero, Player 2 is GM. In Round 2, players swap keyboard & mouse on the exact same layout!

🏆 Victory Conditions

If Player 1 died, Player 2 wins instantly by surpassing Player 1's distance. If both finish the course, the player with the fastest extraction time wins!

⏳ Turn-Based Mode Flow

In Turn-Based mode, the Architect gets a 150 NRG budget to construct traps. The Hero is stopped and waiting until the Architect clicks "CONFIRM & LAUNCH RUN"!

🖥️ Zero-Server 2-Screen Mode

Click "2-SCREEN" to open the GM Blueprint View in a second browser window via native BroadcastChannel. Perfect for dual monitors!
